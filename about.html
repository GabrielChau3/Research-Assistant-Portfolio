Professional Overview

I am an Ottawa-based financial and data analyst who translates rapidly changing macroeconomic and financial datasets into clear, data-driven narratives. With a strong foundation in Commerce (BCom), active study of the CFA Level 1 curriculum, and a focus on high ethical standards and financial modelling, I incorporate practical analytics to support economic research, track market developments, and inform strategic policy decisions.

My technical skills bridge business theory and data management. I use visual tools like Power BI, Power Query, and Tableau to collect, clean, and visualize large-scale datasets. Aligning with the Bank's drive for an innovative, open-minded mindset, I integrate AI assistance and digital tools into my analytical work to improve data productivity and transformation, enhance process management analysis, and extract actionable economic insights with high precision.

To further strengthen my quantitative capabilities, I am completing the Business Intelligence & Data Analyst (BIDA) certification. Combined with my completion of the Canadian Securities Course (CSC), I possess a deeper understanding of equity markets, regulatory environments, and financial modelling, ensuring I can immediately contribute high-level value to the Bank of Canada’s core economic and financial analysis teams.
